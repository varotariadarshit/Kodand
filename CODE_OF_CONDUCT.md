# Code of conduct

Kodand follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus on the
work. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and publishing someone's private
information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any
other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer
privately at the email address on [@varotariadarshit](https://github.com/varotariadarshit)'s
GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove
comments or content, or block participants, who break this code of conduct.
